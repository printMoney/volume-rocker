# Code of Conduct

Volume Rocker follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## In short

We want this to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Harassment, insults, trolling and personal or political attacks are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through a [GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/printMoney/volume-rocker/security/advisories/new) or a direct message on GitHub. All reports are reviewed promptly and confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who violate this code, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
